## 1. Concept / Theory


| # | Resource | Why |
|---|----------|-----|
| 1 | [Recursion and Backtracking — CF blog (yami9)](https://codeforces.com/blog/entry/118843) | Recursion basics, recursion trees, complexity of recursive brute force, ends with a curated problem list (used below) |
| 2 | [[Tutorial] Recursion — CF blog (coris)](https://codeforces.com/blog/entry/92031) | Explicit recursion / "complete search", walks through Climbing Stairs, Combinations, Permutations with recursion trees and pruning |
| 3 | [GeeksforGeeks — Introduction to Recursion](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/introduction-to-recursion-data-structure-and-algorithm-tutorials/) | Linked from resource #1, good for filling gaps in fundamentals |
| 4 | [GeeksforGeeks — Backtracking Algorithms](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/backtracking-algorithms/) | Dedicated backtracking primer: state-space tree, pruning, standard problem categories (N-Queens, Sudoku, subsets, etc.) |
| 5 | [USACO Guide — Introduction to Complete Search](https://usaco.guide/bronze/intro-complete) | Ties recursion into brute force / complete search framing, with practice built in |
